# Photosynthesis

> [!question] Essential question
> How do plants turn light into food?

>> [!cue]
>> What is photosynthesis?
>
>> [!notes]
>> The process plants use to turn light, water and CO2 into glucose and oxygen.

>> [!cue]
>> Where does it happen?
>
>> [!notes]
>> In the chloroplasts, mostly in leaf cells. Chlorophyll absorbs the light.

>> [!cue]
>> Two stages?
>
>> [!notes]
>> Light reactions (make ATP + NADPH) then the Calvin cycle (builds sugar).

> [!summary] Summary
> Plants capture light energy in chloroplasts and store it as sugar, releasing oxygen as a by-product.
